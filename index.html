What is Rift Room?

Rift Room is a free-to-play VR/PC social multiplayer experience built around bringing people together in fun, creative, and immersive ways.

It supports VR and PC cross-play, letting players meet, explore, and hang out in shared virtual spaces.

Development began in November 2025 and continues to grow with new systems, worlds, and features.

The goal is simple: a long-term evolving social universe built around community, creativity, and shared experiences.

Expect constant updates, new environments, and expanding social features over time.
